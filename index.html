(05) / questions

the honest answers

what exactly do i download?

A zip file. Inside it: a print PDF on a light background so it does not drain your ink, a tablet PDF with tappable month tabs, a link free version if tapping annoys you, the loose png stickers, and a one page how to use.

will it work on my ipad?

It is a normal PDF, so it opens in GoodNotes, Notability, Noteshelf and most note apps on iPad or Android. The tabs are PDF links, which these apps support. I test the links before a file goes out, but tablet apps update often, so if a tab misbehaves on your app, email me and I will fix the file.

is it dated?

No. Month names are printed, but there are no years or weekday columns locked to a calendar, so the same file works in 2027 and the year after. February is drawn with 28 days and the 29th is the first hatched square in a leap year.

can i print it at home?

Yes. Pages are US Letter landscape and the print version uses a light background with colour only in the small parts. A4 works too if you print at fit to page.

refunds?

Digital files cannot really be returned, but if something is broken or not what the page described, email me within 30 days and I will refund it. I would rather do that than argue over five dollars.

who makes these?

One person, by hand, page by page. This is a new shop, so there are no reviews here yet. I would rather show you the actual pages than invent praise for them.

can i resell them?

No. Everything here is for your own personal use. Print as many copies as you like for yourself, but please do not share the files or sell them on.
